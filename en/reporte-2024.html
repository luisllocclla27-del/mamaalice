Chapter 02

Programs 2024

Five integrated programme areas working together for deep and lasting change in the Yanama community, Ayacucho.

Education
Education Programme

416 Students · +43 % Academic Improvement

We served 416 students across pre-school, primary, secondary and individual education levels in Yanama. School results showed a +43 % improvement compared to 2023. "Our students are more social and less aggressive. Their self-image has improved. Chances of derailment are much smaller."

64 pre-school students (57 with regular attendance ≈ 90 %). Individual support for students with specific learning difficulties.

Health
Health Programme

106 Health Interventions · Holistic Family Care

We carried out 106 medical and health interventions, including 28 social work cases (8 % of attended families). Preventive activities in hygiene, nutrition and mental health complemented clinical work.

Family Ginkana festivals, positive parenting workshops, music therapy as emotional expression and the cultural activity "A Trip Through Peru".

HTC Gastronomy
Hospitality Training Center

HTC Group VII · 20 Young Professionals Trained

Group VII of the HTC started in October 2023 with 20 students (13 male, 7 female). They trained as waiters, bartenders and cooks at QuinuaQ Restaurant, accumulating real hands-on experience in front-of-house and kitchen.

"The most important goal of the Hospitality Training Centre is 'to create training, internships and employment for young people'. Majority of graduates find a job or continue their studies."

Rock & Water
Sport & Methodology

New Football Pitch & Rock and Water

With support from the UEFA Foundation for Children we laid out a small grass football pitch where children practise football and where Rock and Water training sessions are held — a methodology working on self-control, trust and peaceful conflict resolution.

Rock and Water was additionally funded by the World of Children Foundation for special activities and workshops throughout 2024.
