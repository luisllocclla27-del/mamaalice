Chapter 02

Education in Yanama

83 % promotion rate: 202 of 243 students passed without requiring a recovery program. Annual target exceeded (goal: 70 %).

Education 2025
Key achievement

202 Students Promoted Without Failing

Of 341 enrolled students, 243 attended regularly. 202 passed without needing a recovery program (83 %). 41 entered the recovery program. Zero students failed after the final evaluation stage. Students at "Achieved" level grew from 62 to 164 between the initial diagnostic test and the November evaluation.

Featured project: "I Can Read and Write" (210 children, 4-month literacy program) with final story presentations. Children gained confidence and overcame fear of public speaking.

Chapter 03

Health, Wellbeing & Psychology

Comprehensive bio-psycho-social support to protect children and strengthen their families throughout 2025.

Health
Health Area

75 First Aid Cases & Oral Health Program

A total of 75 first aid cases were attended during 2025, including injuries from falls, headaches, fever, stomach pain, fungal infections, nosebleeds, sore throats, and dog bites. 6 complex urgent medical cases were resolved with 100 % effectiveness: fractures, neurological problems, skin conditions, and suspected STIs.

Oral health: 107 children actively participated with KARIDENT Dental Clinic. 10 children received treatment for advanced cavities. Sexual education workshops for 28 sixth-graders and young people aged 17–23 in the HTC program.

Social & Psychology Area

20 Social Cases & Rock and Water (174 students)

We managed 20 social cases (17 family, 3 individual; 12 successfully closed). In psychology, 14 individuals received individual psychotherapy (11 completed the process). More than 100 children participated in weekly emotional regulation activities, crafts and games.

The Rock & Water program reached 174 students from two partner schools, strengthening self-control, resistance to peer pressure, and bullying prevention.

Chapter 04

Hospitality Training Center — Gastronomy 2025

Elite culinary training in an 8-month residential program with real and immediate job placement.

HTC 2025
Youth Employability

18 Graduates · 12 Employed Immediately

31 young people enrolled; 18 completed the full 8-month residential program (13 male, 5 female) with over 50 hours of weekly intensive practical training. 100 % of completers participated in professional internships, accumulating 576 hours at QuinuaQ Restaurant.

External internships at 11 restaurants across Huamanga: Takuchi Buffet, Wayra Kusi, Alfredo Fusión, Fusiones, Tomasino Pizzería, La Caleta del Pescador, Puerto Qamka Fusión, Altipacha, Manú Cevichería, La Chola, Via Via. Restaurant chefs consistently highlighted the students' strong preparation and discipline.
