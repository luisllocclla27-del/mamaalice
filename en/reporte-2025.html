Chapter 02

Education in Yanama

Educational impact across 341 students: 243 maintained continuous regular attendance, and of these, 202 (83 %) achieved direct academic promotion without needing recovery. Annual target exceeded (goal: 70 %).

Education 2025
Key achievement

202 Students Promoted Without Failing

We supported, educated, and nourished 341 enrolled children and youth at our Yanama center. Of this total student body, 243 students maintained regular, continuous attendance, enabling individual psycho-pedagogical tracking and concrete progress data. In this group with continuous support, 202 students passed directly without needing recovery (83 %), 41 received targeted academic leveling, and zero students failed at the close of the school year. Furthermore, students at the "Achieved" proficiency level increased from 62 to 164 between the initial diagnostic test and the November evaluation.

Featured project: "I Can Read and Write" (210 children, 4-month literacy program) with final story presentations. Children gained confidence and overcame fear of public speaking.

National Educational Context · OECD Report Published: September 2026

Education in Peru — PISA 2025 vs Mama Alice 2025

The latest PISA results, published by the OECD in September 2026, underline the continuing challenges faced by Peru's education system. Among Peruvian 15-year-olds, only 30 % reached at least the basic proficiency level in mathematics, compared with an OECD average of 65 %. In reading, 42 % reached this level (OECD: 69 %), while in science the figure was 47 % (OECD: 74 %).

Results in mathematics and reading declined compared with PISA 2022, reversing gains Peru had made since 2015, while performance in science remained broadly stable. Educational inequality remains an important concern: in science, students from the most socio-economically advantaged quarter of Peru's population scored an average of 78 points higher than students from the most disadvantaged quarter. These results underline the crucial need for complementary educational support for children growing up in disadvantaged communities.

Health, Wellbeing & Psychology

Comprehensive bio-psycho-social support to protect children and strengthen their families throughout 2025.

Health
Health Area

75 First Aid Cases & Oral Health Program

A total of 75 first aid cases were attended during 2025, including injuries from falls, headaches, fever, stomach pain, fungal infections, nosebleeds, sore throats, and dog bites. 6 complex urgent medical cases were resolved with 100 % effectiveness: fractures, neurological problems, skin conditions, and suspected STIs.

Oral health: 107 children actively participated with KARIDENT Dental Clinic. 10 children received treatment for advanced cavities. Sexual education workshops for 28 sixth-graders and young people aged 17–23 in the HTC program.
