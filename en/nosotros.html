Our essence

A conviction that changed everything

Mama Alice was born from the conviction that no young person should have their future limited by their place of birth. In a context where armed conflict left deep scars, our founder, Dutch Frederique Kallen, decided to act.

Today, Mama Alice brings together emotional health support, basic education, and vocational training — each pillar reinforcing the other — with measurable results that speak for themselves.

Our values

What guides us every day

Three principles that define how we work and how we accompany each young person in their process.

Empathy

Each young person is heard, understood, and accompanied from their personal reality, without judgment.

Transparency

Every resource is audited and reported. Our donors are our partners in the mission.

Sustainability

We do not create dependency. We train for professional autonomy and full independence.

Milestones

Our journey

Over a decade building a future in Ayacucho. Scroll down to travel back in time.

2005

Foundation

Mama Alice is established as an Educational and Psychosocial Assistance Project in the rural communities of Ayacucho, led by Frederique Kallen.

2005

Community Center

The first psychosocial support and tutoring center opens, providing refuge and education to more than 500 children and adolescents.

2018

Hospitality Center

The Vocational Training Center in gastronomy and hospitality is born, forging key alliances with the region's growing tourism sector.

2022

85% Job Placement

By 2022, Mama Alice had achieved an 85% job placement rate among graduates of its vocational training program. The organization also strengthened its innovative model by connecting vocational training directly to its own hospitality-based social enterprise, providing young people with practical work experience and a pathway into formal employment.

2025

20,000+ Lives

Over 20,000 children, young people and families have been guided, supported and accompanied through Mama Alice's programs — a testament to two decades of commitment to Ayacucho's communities.

🏆

Awards & Recognition

Mama Alice has received international recognition including the World of Difference Award, the World of Children Education Award, and the Transparency Prize (Netherlands) — among other prestigious honours.
